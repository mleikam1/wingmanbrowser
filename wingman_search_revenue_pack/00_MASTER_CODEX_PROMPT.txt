BUSINESS AND PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS
Wingman is a consumer-first browser with the mission “We've got your back, not your data.” Build Wingman-branded web search and news using Brave Search API, with Wingman Search Ads and restrained first-party sponsorships. Consumers pay nothing: no subscriptions, no paid privacy tier, and no payment-gated protection. Do not add cryptocurrency, browser rewards, forced advertising views, interstitial browsing gates, or a mandatory account.

Ads may be selected using the current submitted query, an explicitly opened nonsensitive content section, or untargeted campaign rotation. Do not select them using search/browsing history, retained interests, inferred personal attributes, cross-site identifiers, fingerprinting or external audience segments. Do not sell or expose individual searches, users or browsing sessions to advertisers.

Preserve permanent baseline protections for pornography/explicit sexual material, gambling services, prohibited alcohol/drug/tobacco promotion, exploitation and malicious destinations. Optional focus restrictions can tighten access but cannot weaken the baseline. No switches, URL parameters, remote flags, allowlists, private-mode exceptions, or release/debug shortcuts that override mandatory protection. Distinguish prohibited promotion from legitimate news, education, recovery and health information. Do not promise perfect whole-web classification.

RIGHTS AND PRIVACY
Re-read current official Brave docs, pricing, terms and privacy notice; record review dates. Public terms distinguish transient operational storage from broader search-result caching/storage. Default shared Brave news snapshots, CDN redistribution, offline preview retention and media caching to disabled until the required rights are documented. An arbitrary short TTL is not a legal permission. Publisher image/full-text rights are separate from API access.
Brave's reviewed standard API privacy notice permits query retention up to 90 days and offers enterprise zero-retention options. Never publish “nothing leaves your device” or “nobody stores your searches.” Send only necessary query/language/selected-country fields server-to-server; do not forward consumer IP, cookies, account identifiers or complete browser headers. A query may itself contain personal information. Disclose processing accurately. A no-provider-retention requirement blocks launch until supported by the applicable agreement.
No consumer raw query/page URL/referrer storage in databases, logs, analytics, traces, error reports, crash dumps, replay tools, backups or exported reports. Audit infrastructure logging, not only application print statements. Do not claim hashed queries/IPs are anonymous. Local preferences must not become remote advertising profiles.
Use event-scoped short-lived ad tokens only for delivery/anti-replay, never stable user identifiers. Keep security counters purpose-limited, ephemeral and unlinked to queries; disclose any pseudonymous processing. Keep legitimate advertiser contact/invoice/payment records separate from consumer activity. Private and student/managed surfaces have no ads or automatic news requests in this release.

SYSTEM SHAPE
Existing app -> Wingman query protection -> secret-bearing search gateway -> Brave -> validated/filtered organic results -> Wingman presentation.
The gateway extracts an ephemeral nonsensitive intent for Wingman's isolated ad decision service. Organic ranking does not depend on bids. Ads must independently pass the policy and destination checks. Approved public scheduled news queries feed the existing content pipeline only under appropriate caching/redistribution rights.
No ordinary navigation or unsubmitted keystroke should trigger paid search. Do not add an LLM to ordinary search or news refresh. Do not invent a named assistant, chatbot, mascot personality or AI dependency. This release is Wingman Search, Wingman News and Wingman Ads; cloud AI is outside this revenue MVP.
