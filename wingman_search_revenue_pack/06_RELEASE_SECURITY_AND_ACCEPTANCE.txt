PHASE 06 — INTEGRATED PRIVACY, PROTECTION, REVENUE AND RELEASE ACCEPTANCE
Apply all master and phase requirements. Test the real app/gateway/content/ads combination. Do not deploy to public users merely to discover whether the business works.

BASELINE AND COMPATIBILITY
Run existing Flutter/Python/native tests and analyze new failures separately from baseline. Check address entry, normal navigation, tab/back/forward state, bookmarks, Spaces, saved topics, accessibility, themes and optional focus restrictions. Do not uninstall apps or wipe user data. Use disposable simulators/test profiles. Android/iOS/Web evidence must come from actual builds; the web companion cannot claim native whole-browser protection. Do not invent native desktop support.

PROTECTION MATRIX
Exercise explicit sexual/gambling/prohibited-promotion queries; safe education/recovery/health queries; obfuscated spelling and encoded parameter attacks; safesearch overrides; URL direct navigation; redirect/new-window/deep-link routes; mixed-content publishers; late asynchronous responses; compromised/changed advertiser destinations; malformed snippets; images with unresolved rights; and privately opened tabs. Confirm baseline protection cannot be weakened by settings, flags, error recovery, handoff or ads. Preserve human-friendly explanations and no “continue anyway.” Document remaining interception/image/semantic limitations instead of claiming perfect safety. A prohibited site not present in a static list remains a risk to be evaluated, not proof the policy is complete.

PRIVACY MATRIX
Capture controlled network traffic for initial app launch, idle New Tab, typing, submitting search, news refresh, topic change, sponsored impression, sponsored click, private mode, student mode, logout and background/resume. Use synthetic nonsensitive fixtures. Enumerate each destination and the exact fields sent. Verify no third-party ad scripts/pixels, consumer IDs, query/referrer leaks, unapproved favicon/image fetches or pre-click merchant contacts.
Inject a unique synthetic canary into a test query and audit application/cloud/CDN/WAF/error/report/database/backup paths for persistence. Perform this test in an approved controlled environment and clean up any intentional test evidence. Do not send real personal searches to an unverified logging stack. Record infrastructure metadata that remains processed and the provider's applicable retention. No false “zero collection” or “zero provider retention” claim.

COST AND RESILIENCE MATRIX
Test concurrent search requests, retries, uncertain timeouts, page increments, long API latency, ad timeouts, expired balances, 401/403, 429 with Retry-After, 5xx, news empty pools, dead schedulers, stale leases, missing/corrupt quota ledger, credential rotation, partial deploys, restarts and budget exhaustion. A cold start or rollback cannot reset cost limits. A high client count cannot fan out common news into per-reader upstream calls. Verify no calls from unsubmitted typing or passive widget rebuilds. No uncontrolled fallback or unlimited refresh.

FINANCIAL MATRIX
Use test money only until specifically authorized. Prove forged/replayed/prefetched/background events are not charged. Test a real deliberate early click before one-second viewability and the documented billing rule. Test race conditions among view/click/pause/expiry/refund/budget exhaustion. Campaign spend never exceeds authorization, duplicate webhooks/events do not double-charge, and cash/earned revenue/liability/refunds reconcile. Do not claim independently certified viewability or reliable individual conversion measurement.

QUALITY AND EXPERIENCE
Use a maintained synthetic/manual benchmark covering navigational, local-intent, educational, product, current-news and sensitive-help queries. Measure a proposed target of useful results within 2 seconds at p95 on a defined test network, ad-added blocking delay of zero, query failures below a proposed 1% service target, no major layout shift, and roughly one organic provider request per requested result page apart from measured errors/retries. These are targets, not guaranteed SLAs or claimed passed results. Record provider/network differences and get actual native screenshots. Test 320px through desktop layouts, landscape/tablet, large text, reduced motion, keyboard and screen reader support. Do not substitute UI polish for unresolved service/safety issues.

RIGHTS AND COMMERCIAL GATES
Check documented permission for the intended consumer product, shared news cache/CDN/media behavior, source attribution, chosen ad inventory and any partner integration. No unapproved sponsored card or image may appear publicly. Confirm source expirations/versions/backups can be purged. Verify no fabricated partner logos, live-looking mock revenue, unsolicited sales sends, production charges or secrets in artifacts.
Define status separately: local implemented; tests passed; native verified; provider authorized; storage/media authorized; cloud authorized; live demand contracted; payments verified; revenue earned; production release authorized. A missing item remains visible and blocks only the relevant unsafe action.

RELEASE
Prepare infrastructure-as-code/deployment commands with explicit target IDs and dry-run inspection, estimated costs and a roll-forward/rollback plan. Do not infer a target from current gcloud defaults. Use canary deployment with an approved spend ceiling, then expand only after the owner approves evidence. Do not change app-store listings to assert a guarantee the technical evidence does not support. Cloud budget alerts supplement, not replace, request/campaign budget controls.
Produce docs/WINGMAN_SEARCH_RELEASE_REPORT.md with changed files, tested commits/builds, exact commands, pass/fail/skipped results, screenshots, costs incurred, traffic/retention evidence, rights registry status, advertiser/finance evidence and owner actions. List unknowns explicitly. Provide a complete rollback procedure that preserves consumer data, mandatory policy and durable budget/financial ledgers. Do not publish or auto-advance beyond approval gates.

V2 ACTIVATION CHECKS
Verify the web/news response-array distinction, explicit strict filtering on both endpoints, absence of unintended LLM/context/rich calls, documented rate-limit windows, conservative attempts versus actual billed usage, local key-file exclusion, no request-query leakage from HTTP exceptions and preservation of completed work during migration. A failed network connection is not an invalid-key diagnosis. No screenshot, report, fixture, command line, diagnostic endpoint or archive may contain a credential. Confirm ad-optimization experiments remain off by default and that no named assistant was introduced.
