PHASE 03 — BRAVE NEWS, SHARED DELIVERY AND CONTENT RIGHTS
Apply the master prompt and previous phases. Extend backend/wingman_content/ and the actual Home/Discover screens. Do not replace existing feed preferences, approved sources or the protected browser.

1. Add a BraveNewsProvider to the existing NewsProvider/CompositeNewsProvider path. Use GET https://api.search.brave.com/res/v1/news/search. Revalidate the official news schema and read its top-level results array, not web.results. Set safesearch=strict explicitly even though the documented news default is strict. News count supports up to 50; keep a measured bounded count and do not automatically request more pages. See BRAVE_API_CONTRACT.md. It is a query-based news API: do not invent a “top headlines” endpoint or assume a universal category parameter. Use reviewed public editorial query templates mapped to Wingman's existing canonical categories. Preserve current category/preference IDs and sources; inactive/new provider availability must not erase saved user choices.

2. Separate two products: explicit News Search for a user's submitted query, and common scheduled news feeds based on fixed public topics. Neither should be derived from private browsing history. Shared feed schedules are identical for all readers of the same configured country/language. Topic choices and ordering remain local; a current section may provide nonsensitive ad context, but do not retain it as a user interest. Never build or query a per-user feed profile. Student/private/handoff/inactive/Updates-off states make no automatic news calls and reject late callbacks.

3. Production shared Brave news caching/fan-out stays disabled unless the rights register documents permission for that use. A sample plan is eight common feeds, one request each every two hours: 96 attempts/day and 2,880 per 30 days, or $14.40 at $5/1,000 before other charges. This is arithmetic, not a quote for extended storage rights or proof of permission. Preserve existing reviewed sources while rights are unresolved. Do not evade a missing grant with per-reader requests, a “temporary” CDN cache, screenshots, a downstream archive, or an undocumented proxy.

4. When authorized, use one scheduled writer/queue, shared due times, leases/fencing and the durable attempt ledger. No per-app-open Brave refresh. An app refresh reads only Wingman's authorized snapshot. Stagger due work, cap each tick, prevent concurrent writers, do not catch up all missed polls, honor backoff and keep setup/tests within budget. Use a separate finite news allowance and release budget first for user-requested search. Disable duplicate Currents/Brave polling for the same editorial role after successful cutover; keep a reviewed provider fallback rather than paying two services for every query.

5. Store only fields allowed by the agreement: original title, permitted excerpt, publisher identity, canonical link, source identifier, publication/discovery/retrieval times, categories, attribution, expiration, and approved thumbnail metadata. Do not turn API snippets into a full-text archive or fabricate a synopsis. Track publishedAt, discoveredAt and fetchedAt separately; provider freshness can reflect discovery rather than article publication. Unknown time stays unknown. A refreshed snapshot must not relabel an old article as breaking or extend rights beyond the grant.

6. Deduplicate canonical URLs and recognize story clusters without merging distinct publishers' copyrights/assets. Balance source/category coverage and reject clickbait/spam through transparent editorial criteria; do not secretly rank political viewpoints or payers. A finite “You're caught up” feed and explicit Load More are preferable to endless refresh. Preserve sources, publication time, source links and sponsored labels. Existing NewsUSA sponsored status is editorial disclosure, not an earned-revenue entry.

7. Image policy: only the article-associated image supplied through an approved source/contract may be shown, and only when its display/proxy/cache rights are recorded. API access does not automatically grant photo/full-article rights. Unknown rights -> useful text card, not guessed stock art, unrelated image, scraped Open Graph image or generated news photo. Never display publisher logos as fictional partners. Copyrighted full articles must open the original publisher through the existing protected browsing flow; preserve any separately authorized native NewsUSA reader.

8. Media processing must not be an open URL fetch service. Use reviewed source hosts and opaque/signed asset identifiers; no user-supplied URL passthrough. Validate DNS/IP at connect time and every redirect, forbid private/link-local/loopback/metadata endpoints, limit redirects/bytes/time/dimensions, verify MIME, reject active formats or sanitize safely, strip cookies/auth/referrer and exclude raw request URLs from logs. Serve approved images first-party only where authorized. Do not assume a proxy removes copyright obligations or makes all tracking impossible.

9. Rights-aware retention applies to public snapshots, CDN layers, local app caches, offline previews, object versions, soft deletion and backups. Expiration is based on the original authorized retrieval, not reads/republication. Purge revoked/expired records and derivatives. Continue cleanup when the provider is paused. Save explicit user bookmarks as links rather than permanent syndicated content copies. Keep the spending ledger outside content cleanup.

10. Test absent key, missing rights, empty response, malformed HTML, wrong source image, old/future timestamps, conflicting source rights, concurrent jobs, quota failure, no duplicate scheduled fetch, revocation and private-mode transition. Show provider diagnostics by aggregate category/health, not user requests. Demonstrate real current content separately from fixtures and document images intentionally omitted because rights are unresolved.

Deliver implemented adapter/UI, category and provenance mapping, schedule/cost model, rights/retention tests, production gates, cutover/rollback guide and proof that 10,000 clients reading the same authorized snapshot do not cause 10,000 Brave refresh calls.
