Free, Wingman-branded web search and news with first-party contextual Search Ads, New Tab sponsorship and restrained news placements. No named assistant, consumer subscriptions, paid privacy, behavioral ad profiles or sold browsing histories. Ads do not change organic rankings or permanent protections. Revenue optimization means sustainable earned contribution after delivery costs, within quality and policy constraints.
