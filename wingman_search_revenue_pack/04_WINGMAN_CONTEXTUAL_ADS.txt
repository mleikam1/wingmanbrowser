PHASE 04 — WINGMAN ADS: FIRST-PARTY DELIVERY WITHOUT USER PROFILES
Apply the master prompt. Build a minimal real direct-campaign system, not a fictional ad network or full public auction. No third-party SDK, tracking pixel, arbitrary advertiser JavaScript, automatic affiliate rewriting or paid organic ranking.

INVENTORY AND ELIGIBILITY
Initial placements: one clearly labeled Sponsored search result on eligible commercial searches; one static New Tab sponsor; and one Sponsored news card after six organic cards, at most two per deliberate feed session. The sponsored NewsUSA content count should be considered in total sponsored density. Do not show every slot simply because it exists. No ads on private/student/managed contexts, warnings, block pages, settings, login/payment forms, external publisher pages, sensitive help queries or personal/health/financial-distress topics. No automatic refreshing, floating overlays, autoplay or interstitials. If demand is empty, collapse the slot without dead placeholders.
Do not send precise location, IP, advertising ID, prior queries, page history, account ID, session fingerprint or retained interests into ad selection. Match against the current submitted nonsensitive query in memory; use reviewed exact/phrase/semantic intent rules, not raw keyword substring guessing. Finance/health/recovery and other sensitive eligibility exclusions happen first. A broad current-page category or user-selected country may be used only when documented; no inferred demographic segments.

CAMPAIGN MODEL
Create advertisers, campaigns, creatives, approved landing destinations, dates, placements, country/language, contextual targets, negative targets, currency, billing type, bid/rate, prepaid balance, budgets, impression/click caps, approval state, agreement reference and audit metadata. Business contact/billing records are allowed but remain isolated from consumer traffic. Do not pre-populate real brand logos or pretend those brands are clients. Fixtures must be clearly labeled and impossible to activate in production.
Support prepaid fixed-rate CPC and fixed-rate impression packages initially; skip a speculative second-price auction. Put quality/policy/relevance ahead of paid amount. Use bounded weighted rotation/pacing where appropriate, with no organic-rank influence. Reject disallowed products and misleading claims even when a customer offers more money. Landing URL changes, creative changes or newly introduced tracking require renewed approval.

CREATIVE AND NAVIGATION SECURITY
Use first-party text/raster creatives with approved assets and no scripts, beacons, iframes or third-party font/pixel dependencies. Validate advertiser identity/domain and manually review the offer. Automated scanning is supplemental, not proof of safety. Recheck approved destinations on a schedule, at click and throughout navigation where native enforcement supports it. A review/availability/policy failure pauses ads rather than bypassing protection. Do not force open prohibited external apps.
Use opaque internal landing IDs, not open redirects accepting arbitrary URLs. The server resolves only an approved campaign landing destination. Set no-referrer; never append the user's query, security token, history or stable identifier to the merchant URL. A publisher/campaign-wide source code can be used under a reviewed agreement; no individual cross-site attribution token in the strict first-party mode. Do not silently modify ordinary organic/bookmarked/direct navigation into an affiliate link.

MEASUREMENT AND FRAUD
Differentiate ad opportunity, eligible opportunity, fill, rendered ad, visible impression, click, valid billable event, invalid/refunded event and earned revenue. Proposed billing definition: at least 50% of the creative visible for one continuous second in a foreground active view. This is Wingman's contractual definition, not a claim of independent industry certification. Server response is not a viewable impression. Instrument actual app visibility/lifecycle, no background/infinite-refresh impressions. Local per-campaign frequency counters may remain on-device and must not be uploaded.
Issue cryptographically secure event-scoped expiring delivery tokens bound to a campaign/creative/placement and price version, never a user or query. Verify signatures and one-time event state transactionally. Token validity is short (proposed 15 minutes); retain only minimum anti-replay state until expiration and purge it. A click may occur before a one-second viewability event: define and test whether genuine delivered-click events are chargeable independently, rather than incorrectly calling all early clicks fraud. No billable event from a plain GET/HEAD prefetch/link scan.
Authenticated delivery plus a valid token does not prove a human. Add aggregate burst detection, invalid-traffic review, test/internal exclusions, explicit user-action signals and budget risk limits. No fingerprinting. Any short-lived rate-limit pseudonym must be documented, isolated from queries and deleted on its tight TTL; never describe hashing as anonymization. In the absence of credible fraud evidence, quarantine disputed charges rather than pretend fraud has been solved.

MONEY AND CONCURRENCY
Use integer currency micro-units or an exact decimal scheme. Prevent negative balances, duplicate charges, concurrent overspend, repeated click charging and race conditions at campaign expiry/pause. Reserve capped budget for outstanding delivery tokens and release unused reservations on expiry; settlement never exceeds authorized funds. Re-check active status and approval on settlement with a documented policy for already-issued tokens. Include idempotent refund/adjustment records. Separate cash received, unspent advertiser balance, delivered earned revenue and refunds. Do not record prepaid cash as revenue before delivery.
Keep only aggregate campaign/day/placement financial reporting plus necessary business ledger records, without user query/IP/referrer joins. Restrict/merge small reporting buckets; do not promise unique reach, user-level conversion attribution or individual browsing paths. Campaign audit logs record administrator actions, not consumer activity.

UX AND TESTS
Every paid unit says Sponsored before its headline and exposes Why this ad? with a truthful explanation of current context, data not used, and external-merchant behavior after click. Paid results are visually distinct from organic results; no fake safety/recommendation endorsement.
Test duplicate/replayed/expired/forged tokens, token leakage, prefetch, early legitimate clicks, hidden/background views, pause/revocation, no-fill, concurrent budget race, merchant redirects, creative XSS, privacy transitions and accidental billing of fixtures. Ads must not delay organic search. Provide campaign preview and test mode with zero charges and no real advertiser impressions.

Deliver an authenticated admin workflow, direct campaign adapter, first-party renderer, measurement/settlement path, audit tools, privacy documentation and explicit live-ad gate. Partner adapters remain disabled until actual contracts and APIs exist.

V2 CONTRIBUTION OPTIMIZATION
After policy/relevance eligibility and contracted pacing are satisfied, compare compatible approved candidates using estimated net revenue per actual placement opportunity, not headline bid alone. Use only adequately populated campaign/placement/nonsensitive-context aggregates with conservative smoothing; no raw consumer-query records, user histories or provider-results training corpus. Normalize CPC and impression offers consistently, account for their distinct measurement denominators, and keep direct guaranteed delivery obligations. Unknown performance must be marked uncertain and explored only within bounded budgets. Do not charge the estimate: settle actual contract-defined valid events.

Provide a tested candidate chain: eligible direct campaigns; a separately authorized privacy-compatible partner adapter; optional expressly permitted merchant/affiliate action; otherwise no-fill. This describes architectural slots, not existing demand or permission to connect an undocumented service. Avoid duplicate auctions, impressions, click chains and network fan-out. A lower-revenue approved candidate is preferable to any policy violation.

One search ad remains the default. Build an owner-disabled page-level experiment for at most a second Sponsored unit after three organic results, never more than two on the initial results page. It requires documented demand, no protected/sensitive contexts, stable layout, relevance acceptance and actual evidence of incremental net contribution without material quality/latency harm. Use ephemeral page-level allocation and aggregated outcomes, no persistent experiment identifier. Do not claim user retention was measured without an appropriate privacy-reviewed method. No remote flag can increase these ceilings or weaken baseline protection.

Native display sizes may include a responsive banner or 300x250 card within an already authorized New Tab/news placement. Do not treat alternative sizes as extra inventory, stack them beside a sponsor, or inject ads into external pages. No autoplay, interstitials or paid protection. Add accurate no-fill-reason totals so sales can distinguish no contracts, exhausted budgets, contextual mismatch, policy rejection and timeouts without recording queries.
