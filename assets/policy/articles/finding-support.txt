You do not have to solve a difficult situation alone. A trusted adult, school counselor, health professional, or another person you trust can help you work out a next step. You can start simply: I need help, and I am not sure how to explain it.

In the United States, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline provides support for suicide, mental health, or substance use crises by calling or texting 988 using your phone. If there is immediate physical danger or a medical emergency, use emergency services. Outside the United States, use an appropriate local crisis or emergency service.

Wingman does not place calls, send messages, or connect you to a counselor. These are offline contact details, not a monitored help channel. No one is watching this page for requests.
