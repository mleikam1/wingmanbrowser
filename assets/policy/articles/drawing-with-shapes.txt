Choose a cup, shoe, or houseplant that is already near you. Spend a minute looking before you draw. Find its large shapes: an oval opening, a curved side, a triangle of empty space. You do not need special equipment.

Lightly place the biggest shapes first. Compare their sizes. Add a few smaller details only after the overall object fits on the page. If a line feels wrong, draw a second line beside it and compare. The first attempt can stay visible as part of the process.

Try the same object three ways: with only outlines, with three tones from light to dark, and with your non-dominant hand. Notice which version teaches you something new.

This is an original Wingman practice prompt. The National Gallery of Art collection is listed as an art-study reference, not as the source of this exercise or a live destination approved by Wingman.
