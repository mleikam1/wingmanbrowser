Water is stored in oceans, rivers, ice, soil, the atmosphere, and underground. It moves between these places as liquid, solid, or gas. Sunlight supplies energy and gravity helps drive its movement. The water cycle has many routes, not one fixed loop.

Imagine a drop that lands on a garden. It might run downhill, enter the soil, be taken up by a plant, or return to the air. Another drop may stay frozen for a long time. People also change where water is stored, how it moves, and how clean it is.

Draw a map of an imaginary neighborhood. Include a roof, road, garden, stream, and underground water. Add arrows for several possible paths after rain. Use a different color for places people have changed the route.

Ask what is missing: Does your map show water vapor? Does it show water used by plants? A useful model can be simple while still acknowledging what it leaves out.
